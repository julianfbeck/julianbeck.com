I'm excited to announce **Anify**, my newest iOS app designed for anime fans who want to keep track of their watching journey.

## Why I Built Anify

As an anime fan myself, I found existing tracking solutions either too complex or lacking the native iOS experience I wanted. Anify is built from the ground up with Swift and SwiftUI to feel right at home on your iPhone.

## Key Features

- **AniList Sync** - Connect your AniList account and sync your watch history seamlessly
- **Beautiful Interface** - A native iOS design that feels intuitive and polished
- **Quick Updates** - Mark episodes as watched with a single tap
- **Discovery** - Find new anime to watch based on your preferences
- **Widgets** - Glanceable widgets to see what you're currently watching

## Built for iOS

Anify takes advantage of the latest iOS features including:

- Native SwiftUI design
- Home Screen widgets
- Smooth animations and transitions
- Dark mode support
- Optimized for all iPhone sizes

## Try It Today

If you're an anime fan looking for a better way to track your shows, give Anify a try. I'd love to hear your feedback!
