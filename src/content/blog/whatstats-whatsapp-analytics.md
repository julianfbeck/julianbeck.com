WhatStats lets you explore your WhatsApp chats with beautiful, privacy-friendly analytics. Export a chat, open it with WhatStats, and get instant insights.

![WhatStats preview](/media/whatstats-hero.png)

## Features

- Messages per participant and activity over time
- Emoji usage breakdown and statistics
- Shareable Chat Wrapped summary
- Beautiful charts and visualizations

## Privacy First

All analysis runs locally on your device. Your chat data never leaves your phone, and I never see any of your messages. This was a core design principle from day one.

## How to Use

1. Open any WhatsApp chat
2. Tap the contact/group name → Export Chat
3. Choose "Without Media" for faster processing
4. Open the exported file with WhatStats

If you try it out, I'd love feedback and ideas for new insights to add!
